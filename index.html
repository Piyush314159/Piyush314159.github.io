Bayesian Optimization for Accelerator Parameter Tuning — SuperKEKB / Belle II

Overview

Addressing the challenge of tuning multi-parameter accelerator systems under noisy, black-box objectives by implementing a Bayesian Optimization framework, motivated by injection-tuning methods at SuperKEKB (KEK, Japan). The Belle II experiment operates at the SuperKEKB electron-positron collider, where precise accelerator parameter tuning is critical for optimal luminosity and data quality.

Methodology

Developed a Gaussian Process-based optimizer with EI/UCB acquisition functions, incorporating segmented region search, step-by-step parameter constraints, and proximal biasing to explore noisy, non-convex parameter spaces. Parameter constraints limit each proposed update within the simulated tuning workflow.

Benchmarking & Analysis

Benchmarked against random & grid search and Nelder-Mead on simulated objectives, using SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to quantify per-parameter contribution to optimization outcomes. The ongoing work examines convergence, sample efficiency, and robustness to noise; results describe simulation studies rather than deployment on the operating accelerator.

02

DemandCast — Retail Forecasting & Inventory Planning

Ongoing

Built a configurable Rossmann store-sales forecasting pipeline with LightGBM, SARIMA, Prophet, a seasonal baseline, and an optional attention GRU. Added calendar-day lag and rolling features, training-only preprocessing, MySQL storage, and bottom-up aggregation across available hierarchy levels. A Next.js dashboard explores exported predictions, data checks, and model outputs.

The GRU uses 56 days of history to forecast 14 days. On one fixed-origin historical evaluation across 1,115 stores / 15,610 observations, it achieved 20.134% WMAPE versus 20.920% for a matched weekly seasonal baseline. Broader backtesting remains ongoing. The inventory planner is an assumptions-based calculator; measured replenishment savings have not been established.

03

DigitalTwinAI — Hydraulic Condition Monitoring

Ongoing

Built a Flask dashboard around 2,205 recorded hydraulic test-rig cycles and 14 physical sensor channels. A compact multirate temporal convolutional network (TCN) combines sensor sequences with 140 summary features to estimate cooler, valve, pump, and accumulator conditions. The workspace supports cycle replay, measured traces, confusion matrices, and CSV/JSON exports.

The 12,558-parameter TCN achieved 0.9449 mean component macro F1 across three grouped research folds, versus 0.8957 for the matched linear baseline. The dashboard checkpoint was subsequently fitted on all UCI cycles; independent-machine evaluation remains future work. Source fingerprints, separate reference labels, and missing-data checks keep displayed estimates traceable to recorded inputs.

05

Spam Classifier from Scratch — NLP & ML

Completed

Implemented Multinomial Naive Bayes (MLE + Laplace smoothing) and Logistic Regression (gradient descent) entirely from scratch in NumPy on the UCI SMS Spam Collection (5,572 messages). Built both TF-IDF and Bag-of-Words feature pipelines without sklearn, with vocabulary constructed strictly on the train split to prevent data leakage on an 86.6% class-imbalanced dataset.

Added validation-based early stopping and threshold selection, custom ROC evaluation, and SQLite-backed experiment tracking with per-message predictions and an error-analysis view. Under the corrected evaluation protocol, Naive Bayes achieved 0.9286 F1 / 0.9771 ROC-AUC and logistic regression achieved 0.8442 F1 / 0.9811 ROC-AUC. The historical test set had been inspected in earlier experiments; independent data is needed to confirm generalization.

06

COVID-19 Epidemic Modeling — SIR / SEIR / SUTRA

Completed

Implemented and compared three compartmental epidemic models — SIR, SEIR, and the SUTRA model (which explicitly tracks undetected/asymptomatic carriers via a 'Unreported' compartment) — on US national COVID-19 data from Feb 2020 to Mar 2021. Used NNLS regression for automatic per-phase parameter estimation of contact rate β̃ and reach ρ̃, with a product-R² fallback for collinear phases, achieving MAPE as low as ~4% on individual phases.

Ensembled all three models via linear regression for improved overall fit. ODE systems solved with scipy's RK45 integrator. Visualised phase transitions, reproduction numbers, and ensemble predictions across the full pandemic timeline.

07

Diffraction Pattern Analysis — Wavelength Estimation

Completed

Built a two-script Python pipeline to extract laser wavelength from camera images of diffraction gratings. Stage 1 preprocesses raw frame stacks into 1D intensity profiles via row-wise averaging and background subtraction. Stage 2 fits the full Fraunhofer grating equation to the resolved diffraction peaks via non-linear least squares (scipy.optimize.curve_fit), yielding λ = 524 ± 6 nm — consistent with the known green laser standard.

08

Brownian Motion — Stochastic Simulation & MSD Analysis

Completed

Simulated 2D stochastic particle trajectories under Brownian dynamics using the Langevin equation with Gaussian noise. Computed the Mean Squared Displacement (MSD) ensemble average across thousands of trajectories and fitted MSD ~ tα via log-log regression to classify diffusive regimes: sub-diffusion (α < 1), normal diffusion (α = 1), and super-diffusion (α > 1). Validated against Einstein's analytical MSD prediction (MSD = 2dDt), confirming the diffusion coefficient D within numerical precision.
